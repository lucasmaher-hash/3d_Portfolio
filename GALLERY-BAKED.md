## Pipeline

`W` = a fresh workspace, e.g. `new 3d scene 2/fourth-export-v06` with `final/` and `probes/`. Python steps use the `.bake-runtime` venv there (OpenImageIO).

1. `Blender -b gallery-review-v06.blend --python scripts/bake-gallery-fourth.py -- $W/final` (about 25 min on the M-series GPU). Native DIFFUSE direct + indirect + colour, 256 maximum samples, adaptive .01/min64, full geometry, float OpenEXR ZIP masters. The to.morrow glass button and its icon are exported but not baked (native PBR, like the bottle). `--pilot` bakes only representative receivers.
2. `Blender -b gallery-review-v06.blend --python scripts/render-gallery-probes.py -- $W/probes`: one equirect EXR at the centre and over every podium (positions read from the podiums), each hiding its own project, plus `cameras.json` review views.
3. `scripts/denoise-gallery-hdr.py $W/final` uses Blender's bundled OIDN in scene-linear HDR mode. Original masters remain alongside the filtered ones.
4. `scripts/encode-gallery-hdr.py $W/final $W/final/web-maps`, then copy `web-maps/*.png` into `public/gallery-baked-assets/maps/` (delete maps that no longer exist). Per-map-range RGBM PNGs, DATA textures, not sRGB. The decoder reconstructs `rgb * alpha * range`.
5. The display LUT is unchanged: pinned OCIO config, AgX + Medium High Contrast, exposure -0.862496495, 97³ half-float table (frozen config and creation script in the local fourth-export workspace). `gallery-colour-check.html` compares GPU output with the CPU reference.
6. `node scripts/package-gallery-baked.mjs $W/final`: packages the geometry, binds bake UV metadata and imports the exact bottle from the protected third asset (verified after serialization).
7. `node scripts/swap-gallery-old-exhibits.mjs`, every time after step 6. Replaces the baked Cybercoffee egg and Virtual Cooking panel with the untouched meshes and materials of the old coloured-rooms scene (`public/current🟢.glb`), fitted per vertex onto the baked copies. Their lightmaps are dropped.
8. `python3 scripts/clean-floor-lightmap.py $W/final/web-maps/floor.png`: smooths the lumpy LED glow along every floor edge. Always pass the encoder's untouched output; the script never reads its own cleaned file.
9. `public/gallery-baked-assets/wall-seams.json` comes from the Blender build (`build_v06.py`), not from the GLB: the walls are flat now, and the panel lines are only drawn by `drawWallSeams()` in `src/main.js` (Lucas's values `WALL_SEAM = { dark 0.14, width 0.013, soft 0.45 }`). Rebuild it whenever the walls change.

**Retired with v06:** `extract-wall-seams.mjs`, `raster-wall-seams.mjs` and `clean-wall-lightmaps.py`. They existed only because the 8 mm grooves baked as random lines and blots. The grooves are gone from the geometry, so the wall bake needs no cleaning.

The centre help podium (podium 07) is baked since 2026-10-24: Podium 04's mesh (seams kept) with the straight band 15 cm shorter, its own floor hole and LED ring, lit by the centre well. Its faint top shadow is NOT baked: a physical half-size occluder under the 4 m area light casts almost nothing. `capPodiumTops` reads to.morrow's baked shadow profile off podium 06 and applies it to podium 07 at half the radius and half the depth (`SHADOW_*` constants). The web also adds the chrome "?". The podium-top centre smoothing (`capPodiumTops`, the seams still narrow to slits at the centre) stays web-side by Lucas's choice and runs on podium 07 too.

## Colour and reflection limits

The LUT calibration uses a visible browser test, not only a matching setting name. The small native-versus-baked Cycles pilot separates diffuse fidelity from view-dependent specular. Whole-scene reflection/refraction pixels cannot be identical to Cycles: local environment captures approximate parallax and Three transmission differs from path tracing. Do not compensate with arbitrary wall multipliers. The approved physical lighting is authoritative.

Project navigation and camera-return storage are isolated to the fourth route. The original scene and third route retain their own handlers/storage. Final verification must check the actual packaged scene, bottle semantic manifest, original GLB hash, browser errors, spawn/exit/collision, representative cameras and texture memory.

Verified: production build; 22 receiver UV bindings; all 22 encoded PNGs preserve every RGBM byte after serialization; bottle attributes/materials/textures and oriented triangles match the retained source (Meshopt may cyclically rotate triangle indices); six HDR reflection probes; five project/podium groups; approved movement/spawn; project open and Exit return in browser; walking through the center exit. Per-receiver material instances prevent deduplicated GLB materials from sharing the wrong lightmap uniforms. PNG export explicitly treats the multiplier alpha as unassociated DATA. Files total about 51.9 MB, and lightmap mip allocation alone is approximately 160 MiB; phone performance has not been measured.
