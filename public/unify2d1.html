The pivot

4

I designed the first version: blue, typewriter type, hard lines. It was clear and structured, but the team's feedback round found it felt cold — like a tool, not something you'd share your location in. The information architecture and the timetable logic stayed; only the visual language was replaced. [ Result of the preference test goes here. ]
