ABOUT

Background
+

Hi, I'm Lucas Maher, a 21 yo designer based in Munich.

I've always created with my hands. Long before I discovered design, I was drawing, sculpting clay, cutting glass, welding metal, sewing fabric, and painting in oil and acrylic — constantly switching materials, constantly asking: what can this become?

That restlessness led me to Industrial Design, where three-dimensional thinking meets real-world problems. Now, studying at Hochschule München, I'm channeling that same energy into the digital space — where 2D and 3D collide, and where screens are becoming the new material to shape.

Things I Love
+

What excites me most right now is spatial computing — VR & AR. The idea that digital experiences can exist in three-dimensional space, that you can step inside a design rather than just look at it through a screen, feels like the most radical shift in how humans interact with technology. I want to be at the forefront of that.

Outside of that, I find a lot of inspiration in film — the way directors use composition, light, and pacing to guide attention and evoke emotion isn't so different from what we do as designers. And sport keeps me grounded: the discipline, the consistency, the reminder that good results come from showing up every day.

Design Philosophy
+

Design that only looks good has already failed. I came to this field from fine art and craft — and that background taught me one thing above all: beauty without purpose is decoration, not design.

I work at the intersection of bold aesthetics and real problem-solving. I'm not afraid to experiment, to try what hasn't been done, or to borrow from unexpected places. The best ideas often live at the edges — between physical and digital, between 2D and 3D, between what exists and what could.
